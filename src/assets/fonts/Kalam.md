# Kalam

Handwritten font by Indian Type Foundry, copyright 2014.
Licensed under SIL Open Font License 1.1; see `Kalam-OFL.txt`.

Sources: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/kalam

`kalam_regular.typeface.json` and `kalam_bold.typeface.json` are local
Three.js typeface conversions of Kalam Regular and Bold, retaining Latin
characters and punctuation. Converted with Three.js TTFLoader and
opentype.js 1.3.4. No remote font requests are needed at runtime.
